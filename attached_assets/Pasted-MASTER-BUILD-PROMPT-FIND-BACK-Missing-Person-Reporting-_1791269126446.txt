3. SAFETY & PRIVACY PRINCIPLES
============================================================

This application deals with sensitive personal information.

Implement privacy-by-design.

Public users should NOT see:
- Private phone numbers
- Private email addresses
- Exact private addresses
- Sensitive evidence
- Authority-only notes
- Internal investigation information

Public users can see only information appropriate for helping identify
a missing person.

Authorized users may access additional information according to their role.

Never expose sensitive data simply by hiding it in the frontend.

Use server/database-level authorization and Supabase Row Level Security.

AI must NEVER make a final decision about whether someone is a missing person,
whether a sighting is genuine, or whether a person has been found.

AI can only provide assistance to authorized human reviewers.
